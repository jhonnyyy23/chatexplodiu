CHAT EXPLODIU

The best live stream moments, in vertical.

Chat Explodiu is a personal tool that watches live streams, detects the moments when the chat explodes, and turns them into short vertical clips (9:16) with captions and hashtags.

How it works

  1. The tool follows a live stream and measures chat activity and audio volume.
  2. When a highlight happens, it cuts a short vertical clip.
  3. It writes a caption with hashtags and credits the streamer.
  4. After the creator logs in with TikTok, the clip is uploaded to the creator's own TikTok account.

Only the account owner uses this tool. It never posts to anyone else's account.
