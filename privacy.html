CHAT EXPLODIU

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Summary

Chat Explodiu runs on the owner's own computer. We don't have a server, we don't sell data, and we don't share data with anyone except TikTok, and only when publishing a video you asked to publish.

2. Data from TikTok

When you log in with TikTok, the app receives:

  • Access tokens. They are stored only in a local file on the user's computer and are used to upload videos to your account.
  • Basic account info (user.info.basic): your open ID and display name. They are used only to identify the connected account.
  • Posting settings returned by TikTok, such as the allowed privacy options. They are used only to build the post request.

3. Live stream data

To find highlights, the app processes the video, audio and public chat messages of the live stream being watched. It processes them locally and briefly, keeping only a few minutes of video in a temporary buffer. Public chat messages from a clip may be saved locally to help write the caption. They are never sent to TikTok or anyone else.

4. What we share

The only data sent to TikTok is the video file and the caption you choose to publish. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

5. Retention and deletion

Temporary video segments are deleted automatically. To remove everything, delete the app's local files (including the token file) and revoke Chat Explodiu's access in TikTok: Settings → Security → Apps and services.

6. Children

The app is not meant for children under 13. It is not used to collect children's data.
